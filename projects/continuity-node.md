# Continuity Node

Continuity Node asks how people can preserve life, agency, truthful communication,
and recoverable choices through disruption. The intended outcome is an offline,
interactive knowledge node that helps different people ask useful questions
without surrendering judgment to an opaque tool.

Its purpose is larger than copying a reference library. A useful node also needs
ways to distinguish evidence from inference, retain uncertainty, identify who
has authority, admit correction, and avoid consuming all available attention
with verification. It should build capacity before crisis and remain useful
even when the worst scenarios never happen.

## Implemented experiments

Two examples of the project's synthetic, offline engineering exercises:

- **Verified release activation and rollback:** stage signed packets without
  executing them, activate an eligible newer packet, reject replay/downgrade,
  and recover the exact last-known-good packet using independently pinned
  checkpoints. This does not establish real service health or protection from
  a compromised host administrator.
- **Ambiguous external effect recovery:** kill a subprocess immediately before
  or after a synthetic non-idempotent effect, preserve the distinction between
  not attempted, unknown, applied, and confirmed not applied, and require
  specific evidence and authority for subsequent action. Fresh approval does
  not silently erase uncertainty. This is not a proof of exactly-once production
  execution or safe real-world compensation.

## Human commitments

Tools advise and act within explicit bounds. Affected people can question,
refuse, and contribute local knowledge. Recovery remains possible; uncertainty
is not turned into certainty for convenience. Preparedness should protect
curiosity, attention, and the ability to care for one another—not manufacture
fear or consent.

The full knowledge system, broad adversarial durability, and deployment at scale
remain goals. These experiments are bounded prototypes, not validated medical,
emergency, or security infrastructure. Project source publication is under
review; this page summarizes the work without importing private continuity,
credentials, or operational records.
