# Dillon Ginsburg

I build systems that help people understand what is happening, make useful
decisions, and recover when something goes wrong. I work across Linux, hardware,
networks, automation, and human-directed AI—and I am especially drawn to problems
that do not arrive with an established procedure.

The question behind my independent work is simple: **how can powerful tools help
people think together, trust responsibly, and retain meaningful control when
information or systems become unreliable?** Technology should make people more
capable of helping one another. Kindness, curiosity, and careful engineering
belong in the same room.

## Independent research and systems

- [Continuity Node](projects/continuity-node.md) explores offline knowledge,
  evidence preservation, explicit human authority, and recoverable decisions.
  Implemented synthetic exercises test signed release activation, rollback,
  and recovery from uncertain external effects. The larger accessible knowledge
  node remains a research goal, not a deployed or certified safety system.
- [The Clearing and the cohort](projects/the-clearing.md) make AI-assisted work
  inspectable: durable collaborators, separate interfaces and owners, attributed
  messages, observable status, and human controls. The Clearing has a working
  Linux-native Qt/QML interface; individual features have their own qualification
  and deployment boundaries.
- [FOR/REST](https://github.com/dillonrephan/forrest) is a read-only Godot spatial
  filesystem prototype: bounded navigation, literal path completion, and a
  filesystem represented as a place rather than only a list.
- [Canopy](https://github.com/dillonrephan/canopy/tree/visual/perspective-sakura)
  explores botanical filesystem navigation in C++ and Qt Quick, with asynchronous
  scanning and explicit partial, inaccessible, and loading states.
- [QRator](https://github.com/dillonrephan/qrator) is a music-discovery and
  curation workspace with provider integrations and experimental local analysis.
  Provider behavior and playlist privacy need independent verification.
- [Shelldon](https://github.com/dillonrephan/shelldon) packages a customized
  terminal environment. Its README identifies upstream components and explains
  why the installer should be inspected and existing shell files backed up first.

I initiate and direct this work, define requirements, evaluate designs, and
operate the resulting systems. AI collaborators contribute substantially to
implementation and testing. Repository histories and project notes carry the
specific authorship; this is not a claim that I hand-wrote every line.

## PC Place and professional automation

My foundation is four years of hands-on technical work with individuals and
small businesses: hardware and software troubleshooting, preventative security,
data recovery, networks, and everyday user support. I use shop automation daily
to make recurring diagnostics, deployment, backup, and recovery work more
consistent and inspectable.

That Windows-focused work is a separate professional toolchain, not the center
of my independent research. The maintained PC Place automation and diagnostics
repositories remain private while licensing, customer privacy, and publication
boundaries are reviewed. Empty historical repository placeholders are not
examples of completed tools.

## Working together

I am interested in infrastructure and research environments where dependable
